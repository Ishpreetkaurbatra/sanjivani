FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Market Navigator™ completely free?

Yes. Built by Dream of Soul Foundation, a Section 8 NGO. There are no fees or hidden costs for artisans, SHGs, or MSMEs.

What is GeM and how does this tool help?

GeM (Government e-Marketplace) is India's official public procurement portal. The tool analyzes your product category, generates a readiness score, and details how to win government tenders.

Can women entrepreneurs and SHGs use this?

Absolutely. It is specially optimized for artisan collectives, weavers, women SHGs, and rural producers seeking domestic and overseas growth.
